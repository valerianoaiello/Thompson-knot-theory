## Independent verification of the DIFFERENT cases

`verify_reverse.py` (output in `verify_reverse_log.txt`) lists, for each candidate at the decisive n, all the
elements g and g^-1 of vec{F} in its HOMFLY class, identifies each one with the SnapPy census, and compares
the diagrams of the knot **with each other** (not with KnotInfo). Two diagrams related by 'reverse' or
'mirror' would mean that both orientation classes {K, Kmr} and {Kr, Km} occur.

- Control: 9_33 at n = 13 — both occur (as found by the scan).
- 10_88: at n = 13 only one orientation class (2 elements), at n = 15 both. So one of K, Kr has
  vec{F}-index 13 and the other 15.
- 10_93, 10_102, 11a_26, 11a_30, 11n_67, 11a_76, 11a_103, 11n_132, 11n_177, 11a_256, 11a_270, 11a_287:
  at n = 15 (the first n of their HOMFLY class) only one orientation class occurs, so one of K, Kr has
  vec{F}-index 15 and the other >= 17.
- The "other" diagrams in these classes are different knots with the same HOMFLY (12n_586, 11a_315,
  11a_272, 11a_289), not failures of SnapPy.
